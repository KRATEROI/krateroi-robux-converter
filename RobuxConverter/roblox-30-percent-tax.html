The Roblox 30% marketplace tax explained

Every time you sell something inside Roblox — an item, a game pass, or a developer product — Roblox keeps 30% of the sale. Only 70% reaches you. Understanding this changes how you price things.

How it works

The tax is a flat 30% of the listed price. Sell a game pass for 100 Robux and you keep 70 Robux; Roblox takes 30. It applies to in-platform sales only — simply buying Robux is not taxed.

Pricing to receive a target amount

If you want to actually receive a certain number of Robux, divide your target by 0.7 and round up. To pocket 700 Robux you must price the item at:

700 ÷ 0.7 = 1,000 Robux
