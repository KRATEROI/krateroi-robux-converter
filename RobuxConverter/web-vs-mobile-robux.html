Is buying Robux cheaper on web or mobile?

Yes — buying Robux through the Roblox website gives you noticeably more Robux for the same money than buying inside the mobile app or on a console. Here is why, and how much the difference is worth.

Why the website is cheaper

When you buy Robux inside an iOS or Android app, Apple and Google take roughly a 30% cut of the transaction. Roblox passes that cost on to you by handing out fewer Robux for the same price on mobile and console. Buying at roblox.com in a browser skips the app-store fee, so the same dollar buys more.

How to always pay the web price

  • Buy from roblox.com in a desktop or mobile browser, not the installed app.
  • Redeem Roblox gift cards on the website — they credit the web amounts and often carry seasonal store discounts.
  • For big targets, combine packages efficiently instead of buying one huge pack blindly.
