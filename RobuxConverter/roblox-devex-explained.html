Roblox DevEx: how to cash out Robux for real money

The Developer Exchange (DevEx) is the only official way to turn Robux into real currency. It is built for creators who earn Robux, not for players who buy it. Here is exactly how it works.

The DevEx rate

DevEx pays a fixed US$0.0035 per Robux. In round numbers:

  • 1,000 Robux = US$3.50
  • 30,000 Robux = US$105
  • 100,000 Robux = US$350

This is roughly a third of what the same Robux costs to buy — the spread is how Roblox sustains the marketplace.

Who is eligible?

  • Be at least 13 years old with a verified account.
  • Cash out only earned Robux — from selling items, game passes, or developer products. Purchased Robux does not qualify.
  • Meet the minimum of 30,000 earned Robux per cash-out request.
  • Comply with the Roblox Community Standards and DevEx program terms.

A full payout example

Say a player buys a game pass you priced at 1,000 Robux:

  1. Sale price: 1,000 R$
  2. Roblox marketplace tax (30%): −300 R$
  3. You keep: 700 R$ of earned Robux
  4. At the DevEx rate that is worth about US$2.45 once you are over the minimum

The DevEx tab runs this whole pipeline for you — sale price → 30% tax → net Robux → DevEx payout → your local currency — and warns you when you are still below the 30,000 Robux minimum.

Important

The rate and minimum can change, and payouts are processed to a verified payment account. Always confirm the current terms at create.roblox.com before planning a cash-out.
