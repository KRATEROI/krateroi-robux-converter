Privacy policy

This page explains what information RobuxConverter uses and how. The site is a free calculator; it does not require an account and does not ask for personal details.

Information we collect

RobuxConverter does not collect names, emails, or account data. Your calculator inputs are processed in your browser and are not sent to us. Your chosen language and currency preferences are stored locally in your browser (localStorage) so the site remembers them on your next visit; you can clear them at any time from your browser settings.

Exchange-rate data

To show live currency values, the site requests public exchange rates from a third-party service (ExchangeRate-API). These requests contain no personal information beyond what any web request includes (such as your IP address, handled by that provider).

Advertising and cookies

This site may display ads served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you.

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Where required by law, an on-page consent prompt lets you accept or decline personalized ads.

Children

This site is a general-audience tool and is not directed at children under 13. Where advertising is shown to users who may be minors, it is limited to non-personalized ads in line with the ad provider's policies.

Your choices

You can block cookies in your browser, use the Google opt-out above, or decline consent in the on-page prompt where offered. Blocking cookies does not affect the calculator's functionality.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the site owner via the contact address published on the site. This policy may be updated; the current version always lives at this page.
