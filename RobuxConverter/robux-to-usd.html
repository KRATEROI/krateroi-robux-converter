How much is Robux worth in USD?

The honest answer is that Robux has two different values: what it costs you to buy it, and what you get when you cash it out. They are far apart, and knowing both stops you from over- or under-estimating your Robux.

The short answer

When you buy Robux from Roblox, you pay roughly US$10 per 1,000 Robux. The best value comes from the largest package on the website (US$199.99 for 24,000 Robux), which works out to about US$8.33 per 1,000 Robux.

When a developer cashes Robux out through the Developer Exchange (DevEx), the rate is fixed at US$0.0035 per Robux, or US$3.50 per 1,000 Robux — roughly a third of the buying price. That gap is how Roblox funds the platform.

Why are there two prices?

Buying Robux is a retail purchase, so Roblox keeps a margin. Cashing out is different: only earned Robux (from selling items, game passes or developer products) can be exchanged, and every in-platform sale first loses 30% to the marketplace fee. The DevEx rate then converts what is left into real money. See how DevEx works for the full walkthrough.

What about other currencies?

The figures above are in US dollars, but Robux is bought and valued worldwide. The Robux converter shows the live value in more than 30 currencies — including CLP, EUR, GBP, BRL, MXN and ARS — using up-to-date exchange rates, so you can see exactly what your Robux is worth in your own money.
